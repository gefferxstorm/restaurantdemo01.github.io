A table at the heart of Maison Aurelia Sanur

Tucked inside the hotel garden, the restaurant is a place to gather over honest French cuisine, good wine, and conversations that naturally linger.

We cook what we would want to eat on a slow afternoon: buttery sole meunière, a proper onion soup, a cheese board that takes its time. Everything is made daily, from the baguettes to the crème caramel.

  • Produce from Bali farms and fishermen
  • Over 80 French and natural wines
  • Vegetarian and gluten-free dishes always available

Good wine, poured without ceremony

Our sommelier keeps the list friendly. Ask for something crisp, something red and gentle, or simply say what you are eating.

Every Thursday we open a bottle from a small grower and talk about where it came from. Join us at the bar from 5 pm; there is no booking and no pressure.
